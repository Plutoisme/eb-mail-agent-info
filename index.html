EB Gmail Agent

A personal email assistant operated by its owner for their own Gmail account, using a locally running AI model. This is not a public service and does not accept registrations.

What it does

The assistant connects to Gmail through Google's OAuth authorization and Gmail API. With the owner's authorization it can search and read email, manage drafts and labels, send email, and delete messages.

The requested https://mail.google.com/ scope provides full Gmail access, including permanent deletion. This page does not perform these operations or collect Gmail credentials.

Local processing

The owner runs the agent and AI model locally. Email content and attachments are not sent to external AI services. Google processes Gmail API requests as the email service provider.

Access and control

Gmail access begins only after the owner authorizes the application. The owner can revoke access through their Google Account and remove local credentials and downloaded email data.
